# Assumptions and Limitations

## Assumptions
- Cyberdyne Systems is fictional, with approximately 50 employees and a customer-facing SaaS service.
- Illustrative stack: AWS, GitHub, Google Workspace, Slack, and employee laptops.
- Customer data may include personal information; exact data types, jurisdictions, volumes, and contracts are unspecified.
- Role names are job-function placeholders and may be combined in a small company.
- The assessment horizon is the next 12 months.
- Scores are analyst estimates for a portfolio exercise, not conclusions from interviews, logs, scans, or audits.

## Control status
No control is treated as verified because a policy was drafted. All actions are proposed; implementation and effectiveness require evidence, testing, and owner confirmation.

## Open validation areas
Backup and restore testing; IAM/MFA and privileged access; endpoint inventory and patching; logs and alerts; privacy and data retention; vendor inventory; vulnerability SLAs; and control ownership/evidence.

## Limitations
This is not legal advice, an audit, penetration test, formal assessment of a real environment, or SOC 2 attestation. Framework mapping is high-level. Numerical scoring can hide uncertainty, so document evidence quality and judgment. Target residual ratings are placeholders, not measured outcomes.
